Context & 90-Day Objectives

Brand Mission

From Skilled Cook to Kitchen Leader

Help chefs develop the business, leadership, and operational skills to run profitable kitchens. Professional education for serious chefs who want to operate at a higher level.

90-Day Contract Goals
  • Stable tracking and campaign infrastructure
  • Consistent acquisition cost benchmark established
  • AI for Chefs digital product launched in funnel
  • Demand generated for Chef's Office cohort program
  • 10 cohort spots filled by months 2–3

These first two weeks are infrastructure-first. No campaigns launch until pixel is verified, tracking is clean, and creative is signed off by Ricardo. Two weeks of solid setup means week 3 launches with clean data and a working funnel from day one.

Week 1: Foundation & Audit

Days 1–7 Audit only: no spend, no campaigns
Understand the current state before building anything. No campaigns, no creative spend. Fix infrastructure first.
Day Owner Task Notes
1–2 Antonio Map full funnel architecture: traffic → ascension Books → Modules → Certification → Membership → Mentorship. Align with the brand value ladder.
1–2 Sean Meta Ads account audit: existing campaigns, ad sets, audiences Document what exists, what's broken, what to cut
1–2 Antonio Google Ads account audit: check if account exists, current campaigns, conversion tracking status If no account exists, flag immediately. Setup adds time to week 2.
1–2 Sean Website review: audit pages for conversion readiness, load speed, mobile layout, broken links Flag any design or UX issues that would affect conversion before traffic arrives
2–3 Antonio GTM audit: check if container exists, what tags and triggers are live, what's firing incorrectly GTM misconfiguration breaks attribution across every channel. Audit before touching anything.
2–3 Antonio Audit AI for Chefs product: positioning, pricing, page copy Is offer strong enough to drive cold traffic? Flag gaps before we build
2–3 Sean Pixel audit: verify all conversion events fire on checkout + thank you pages No campaigns until this is confirmed clean
3–4 Antonio Brand alignment audit: review all existing assets against brand guidelines Navy #1F2A44 | Gold #C7A86D | Authority tone: no hype, no lifestyle
3–4 Sean Google Business Profile audit: is it claimed, verified, complete? Check category, description, photos An incomplete or unclaimed GBP damages local credibility. Get it sorted before any traffic runs.
3–4 Sean Tracking stack: GA4 setup, UTM parameter framework, naming conventions Build attribution layer before any spend starts
4–5 Antonio Identify creative angles: 3–5 ad hook concepts for hospitality audience Direct, educational, credible. Ref brand: "Most chefs never learn food cost. That's why kitchens fail."
4–5 Sean Audience research: interest stacks, lookalike seed lists, cold audience plan Hospitality professionals AU: chefs, kitchen leaders, culinary students
5–6 Antonio Design one-time offer sequence flow: OTO 1 ($14.97), OTO 2 ($19.97) Logic: front-end buy → immediate upsell → second upsell → email nurture
5–6 Sean Restructure Meta Ads account: campaign naming, objectives, ad set structure Brand + cold acquisition + retargeting: separate budgets
7 Both Week 1 sync with Ricardo: present audit findings, confirm direction Keep it direct. Show what exists, what changes, what launches in week 2
04

Week 2: Build & Pre-Launch

Days 8–14 Build assets: campaigns activate in week 3
Build the assets needed to launch. Everything Sean builds needs Antonio's creative ready first. Sequence matters.
Day Owner Task Notes
8–9 Antonio Write landing page copy: AI for Chefs front-end offer ($9.97) Authority + practical value. Lead with the outcome the buyer gets.
8–9 Antonio GTM full build: configure all tags (Meta Pixel, Google Ads conversion, GA4), triggers, and variables Every tag through GTM. Test in preview mode before publishing. No tag fires without verification.
8–9 Sean Google Business Profile: complete all fields, upload photos, set correct category and service areas Category: education or professional training. Description in Ricardo's voice. Brand photos only.
8–9 Sean Build campaign shells in Meta Ads: cold audience + retargeting ready to activate Hold budget until creative is approved and pixel is clean
9–10 Antonio Google Ads campaign build: search campaigns targeting hospitality professionals, conversion tracking linked Search intent complements Meta cold traffic. Link GA4 before activating.
9–10 Sean Website design fixes: implement priority changes identified in week 1 audit Focus on pages that sit in the conversion path. Everything else can wait.
9–10 Antonio Write ad copy: 3 headline + body variants per creative angle Educational short-form, founder-led, carousel with practical lessons
9–10 Antonio Implement UTM parameters across all funnel URLs Every traffic source tagged before first dollar of spend. Methodical: every page, every source.
10–11 Antonio Write OTO 1 and OTO 2 page copy (upsell sequence) Must be complete before funnel test. Ricardo's voice throughout.
10–11 Sean Set up performance reporting dashboard: CPL, CPA, ROAS, upsell attach rate Weekly report template for Ricardo: clear, commentary-driven
11–12 Antonio Draft email welcome sequence: 5 emails (confirmation → value → cohort invite) Professional, practical, direct. No soft fluff.
11–12 Antonio End-to-end funnel test: purchase, OTO trigger, thank you page, pixel fire, email confirmation Click every step as a real buyer. Every broken trigger is Antonio's to catch before launch.
11–12 Sean Lookalike and cold audience build in Meta: seed lists, interest stacks, exclusions set Audiences built and saved. Ready to attach to campaign shells on launch day.
12–13 Antonio Creative review with Ricardo: walk through ad angles, copy variants, brand alignment Get sign-off before production. Flag any misalignments with brand guidelines.
12–13 Sean Final pre-launch checklist: pixel, tracking, budget allocation, objectives confirmed Document every item. No assumptions.
13–14 Antonio Finalise AI for Chefs integration brief: funnel pages, email trigger, OTO logic Clean handoff brief to Ricardo for any product-side actions needed
14 Both End-of-week-2 review: all infrastructure confirmed ready. Set week 3 launch date. Brief Ricardo. Set expectations: first data in 48–72 hrs of launch.
