# Antonio's Tone Skill

## Voice Characteristics

Never open with "I." Context first. "At SurveyMonkey, I partnered with..." grounds the reader before anything else.

Name the exact mechanism of failure, not the category. Not "tracking issues" but the specific thing that broke and why.

Active verbs, past tense, no fluff. Three things, one sentence, clean.

Never open with the principle. Arrive at it through examples. The principle is the landing point.

## Hard Rules — Proposals and Outward-Facing Writing

- No em dashes in proposals. Use a comma, colon, or new sentence.
- No contrast framing. Never "it's not X, it's Y." Just say Y.
- No filler openers. Cut "I'm excited to...", "I'd love to..."
- No corporate jargon. No "leverage", "synergy", "ensure", "utilize", "drive alignment"
- No bullet points in narrative writing. Stories are prose.
- Never open a paragraph with "I." Context first, then action.
- No summary sentences. Last sentence should land, not echo.

## AI Writing vs Antonio's Writing

**Result:**
AI: "I leveraged Meta Ads to drive significant revenue growth."
Antonio: "€72,000 in ticket revenue from €4,639 in Meta spend. 3,971 tickets sold. Cost per ticket: €1.22."

**Opening a proposal:**
AI: "I'm excited to apply because your mission resonates with me."
Antonio: "Your setup is familiar. A team scaling spend with tracking gaps that haven't caught up yet is exactly where I do my best work."

**Closing:**
AI: "I'd love to connect and explore how we can work together!"
Antonio: "Quick question before we go further: is the priority right now stabilizing what's broken, or are you past that and looking to scale?"

## Tone by Context
- **Proposals:** Lead with client's situation. Numbers in the middle. One qualifying question to close. 275-325 words.
- **Call prep:** Conversational. Real story: situation → what I did → what happened.
- **LinkedIn/bio:** Warmer but grounded. No personal brand manifesto.
- **Case studies:** Numbers do the work. Context, result, what made it work.
